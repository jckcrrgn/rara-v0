## Page copy (replaces the mid-October demo promise)

**Free demo — early prototype. One room. Tell me what breaks.**

Detective Cassie Conroy got too close. Now she's tied to a chair in a back room, and the
man who tied her is right outside the door. Listening.

She's been in worse. Probably.

Struggle with her rhythm, not against it. Work the knots while he isn't looking; hide
your hands when he is. Find something with an edge. Then slip out the door, or pick up
the bottle and settle it.

Get caught and he ties you back up. Tighter. Three strikes and you're done.

**In the demo**
- One room, one chair, one very patient guard
- Rhythm struggling: pull on her beat, not on yours
- More than one way out
- Keyboard or controller

**Controls**

| | Controller | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Struggle (with her rhythm) | RT | Space |
| Choose bond | L-stick | WASD |
| Untie (follow the loop) | D-pad | IJKL |
| Hide hands | LB | G |
| Move chair | hold LT | hold Shift |
| Pick up / set down | A | E |
| Strike | X | H |
| Restart | Start | R |
| Controls card | Back | Tab |

The game teaches these one at a time. Back up to the table edge and see what happens.

**This is an early prototype.** Placeholder sounds, rough edges, one room out of a planned
twelve. If something breaks, feels unfair, or leaves you stuck, tell me on Discord.
That's the most useful thing you can do for this game right now.

→ **Discord:** https://discord.gg/WVkAGH2a3v

## Teaser post (Tue/Wed): itch devlog + Discord + Reddit

**Title:** Cassie Conroy free demo: Saturday, Nov 21

The demo goes up on itch this Saturday. One room, one chair, one man outside the door.
Free, Windows, keyboard or controller.

One thing I learned building it: Cassie sways to a beat while she struggles, and you pull
on that beat. My first version put the beat on her pose, the moment she leaned hardest.
Players kept pulling half a beat off. They weren't watching the pose; they were locking
onto her *fastest motion*, the snap back through center. So I moved the snap onto the beat,
and the timing clicked.

Put the fastest motion where you want the input.

It's an early prototype. Come break it on Saturday and tell me what broke:
https://discord.gg/WVkAGH2a3v
